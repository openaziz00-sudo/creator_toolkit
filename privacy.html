Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Creator Toolkit is designed as a local-first developer tool. This policy explains what data it processes when you choose to connect a platform.

1. Data we process

  • OAuth tokens, client identifiers, and configuration values that you enter in your local environment.
  • Content and performance data returned by an official platform API when you run a sync, report, queue, or publishing command.
  • Local queue, analytics, and campaign records needed to provide the requested features.

2. How data is used

Data is used only to authenticate your requested API calls, synchronize analytics, generate reports, manage your queue, and publish or upload content when you explicitly run those functions.

3. Storage and sharing

By default, data is stored on your device in the local Creator Toolkit directory and SQLite database. The project does not intentionally sell personal data. Data is sent to a connected platform only as required by the API operation you initiate. Platform providers may process data under their own policies.

4. Security

Keep your .env, local database, OAuth files, and API credentials private. Use file permissions and device security appropriate for your environment. No software can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete local records and tokens by removing the Creator Toolkit data directory or using your operating system's file tools. Third-party copies and platform records are controlled by the relevant provider.

6. Children's privacy

The software is not directed to children. Do not use it to process a child's data in violation of applicable law or platform requirements.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the project develops. The current version will remain available on this page.

8. Contact
